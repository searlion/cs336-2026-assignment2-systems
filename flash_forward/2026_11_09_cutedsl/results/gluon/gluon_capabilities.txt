For comparison, Triton's standard tl language on sm_89 (results/triton_v5/):
- One ttg.async_wait %K, %V {num = 0} per iteration waits for K and V together, and the SASS loop has
  DEPBAR.LE SB0, 0x0 and two BAR.SYNC per iteration (loop_sync_counts.txt): no per-buffer waits,
  no mbarriers for cp.async, and tl.range/num_stages set one depth for all loads in a loop.
- tl.range(..., warp_specialize=True) compiles on sm_89 but creates no ttg.warp_specialize region
  (still 4 warps); it changes the kernel (shared memory 40,960 -> 24,576 B, spills 2 -> 10), i.e.
  it switches off the loop's multi-buffering (warp_specialize_probe.txt). Its docstring
  (language/core.py:3281) says warp specialization is only supported on Blackwell.
- The coarse form of v7 (loop rotation: compute S_{j+1} before the softmax of S_j) IS expressible in
  tl: kernels/t6_rotated.
